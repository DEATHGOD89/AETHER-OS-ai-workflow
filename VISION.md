# VISION.md — Aether Product Manifesto

> **One workspace for everything you create with AI.**  
> *Aether remembers your entire project context so you don't have to.*

---

## 1. Why Does Aether Exist?
General-purpose AI tools today suffer from **context fragment syndrome**. Creators copy and paste code, prompts, brand guidelines, and assets back and forth between ChatGPT, Cursor, Canva, Figma, and local code editors. 

Aether exists to eliminate context switching. It shifts the primary unit of AI interaction from a **temporary chat prompt** to a **persistent project**.

---

## 2. Who Is It For?
Aether is built for **Solo Creators, Indie Hackers, Developers, and Product Designers** who want to finish complete projects—from concept and brief to code, visual assets, and deployment—without opening 10 disconnected tools.

---

## 3. What Problems Will Aether Solve?
- **Context Loss**: Aether remembers all project files, design tokens, RAG knowledge, and past decisions across sessions.
- **Workflow Fragmentation**: Provides a single unified operating system workspace containing AI Co-pilot Chat, Code Workbench, Visual Asset Studio, and Vector Memory.
- **Invisible Reasoning**: Explains AI context and evidence through the **Context & Evidence Inspector** instead of hiding behind a black box.
- **Goal Completion**: Active **Project Brain** tracks completion metrics, lifecycle stages (`Brief` → `Research` → `Design` → `Build` → `Review` → `Publish`), and next actionable steps.

---

## 4. What Problems Will Aether Intentionally NEVER Solve?
- **Not a Generic AI Chatbot**: Aether will never try to be a general Q&A assistant for trivia or unstructured web searches.
- **Not a Raw AI Model Marketplace**: Aether does not compete on benchmark scores; it competes on helping creators finish work.
- **Not a Disconnected Tool Collection**: Aether will never feature separate standalone pages for text, image, or video generation. Everything exists inside the single project workspace.
- **Not an Enterprise Admin Form Builder**: Aether will never sacrifice desktop-class speed (`120 FPS` UI, `< 1s` streaming) for bloated corporate configuration forms.

---

## 📍 Long-Term Compass
> *"Aether does not compete to be the smartest AI. It competes to be the best place to finish work with AI."*
